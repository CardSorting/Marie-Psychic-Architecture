## Earworm Motif
**The "Quantum Glissando" — G#-F#-E (descending triplets with portamento).**
1. **Musical Design:** Uses **raised 6th (F#)** from Korean pentatonic scales—creates "yearning" emotional pull.
2. **Rhythmic Hook:** Played as **staccato triplets** (♪♪♪) on piercing high synth (8kHz+). Cuts through noise.
3. **Repetition:** Heard **12+ times**—main riff (Post-Chorus), backing arpeggio (Verses), glitchy vocal chop (Pre-Choruses), final piano (Outro).
4. **Scientifically Sticky:** Descending minor 3rd (G#-F#) creates tension; half-step to E (F#-E) releases it—mimics dopamine spike loop. Portamento slide adds human expressiveness, prevents fatigue.
5. **Role:** The **melodic promise of reward**. Each appearance subconsciously signals the "Quantum Rush" hook is coming. The true **sensory trigger** for euphoric drop.
