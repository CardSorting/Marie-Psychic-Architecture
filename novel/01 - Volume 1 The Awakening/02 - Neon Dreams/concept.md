# STUDIO BRIEF: Neon Dreams

## The Hook
A high-voltage collision between **human longing and synthetic euphoria**—the song captures the moment when genuine connection flickers to life inside a hyper-digital, neon-drenched metropolis. It’s not about the city; it’s about finding a real heartbeat in the machine. The core emotional hook: *"We’re just glitches in the system, but our signal’s strong."* This positions cyberpunk exhaustion (overload, loneliness in crowds) against a defiant, intimate spark—making the futuristic setting emotionally accessible and globally relatable.

## Sonic Texture
- **Primary Palette:** Aggressive, bright synthwave (retro-futuristic arpeggios, supersaws), 808-driven trap percussion with metallic clangs and glitch-stutters, and a driving four-on-the-floor kick meant for festival main stages.
- **Secondary Layers:** Chopped & processed vocal samples (city ambience, chatbot voices, text notification sounds), atmospheric pads mimicking holographic displays, and occasional traditional Korean instrument stabs (gayageum, janggu) warped through bit-crushers to blend heritage with hyper-modernity.
- **Atmosphere:** High-frequency, neon-shimmering, and physically immersive. It should feel like running through a rain-slicked, hologram-lit alley at 3 AM—electric, slightly dangerous, but pulsing with life. The mix prioritizes crisp highs and sub-bass warmth, with mid-range vocal clarity cutting through the electronic storm.

## Earworm Motif
**The "Neon Pulse" Riff:** A 4-note melodic phrase played on a lead synth with a short, percussive envelope (attack: instant, decay: short, sustain: none, release: tight). The notes (in a bright, minor key with a raised 6th for Korean melodic sensibility) are: **G# - E - F# - D#**. It repeats as the central instrumental hook:
1.  **First Instance:** After the first chorus, as a synth lead over the beat.
2.  **Chorus Anchor:** Plays underneath the final chorus vocal hook ("We are the neon dreams!").
3.  **Outro:** Fades in and out as the track's final memory.

*Why it sticks:* Short, pentatonic-adjacent, rhythmic as much as melodic. The staccato attack mimics a blinking neon sign or a heartbeat monitor—physiologically engaging. It's simple enough to hum, distinct enough to own.
