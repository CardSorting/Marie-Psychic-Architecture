# ZENITH STRATEGY: The Inevitability of AGI Gravity Wells

## Signal Cluster
- Compute scaling laws (Chinchilla, Kaplan) proving mass unlocks capability
- Capital concentration: hyperscalers (Google, Meta, Microsoft) spending $70B+ annually on AI infrastructure
- Infrastructure moats: custom silicon (TPU, Trainium), exclusive power/water contracts, proprietary data center architectures
- Data network effects: more users → more interactions → better model → more users (reinforcing loop)
- Regulatory moats: compliance burdens, safety certifications, government partnerships that exclude newcomers
- Talent gravitational clustering: elite researchers consolidate in 3-4 labs
- Inference cost collapse: once-prohibitive deployment now pennies per million tokens, enabling mass adoption
- Alignment research becoming compute-bound, favoring resourced incumbents

## Cosmic Metaphors
- **Agents** → *Coordination Gravity*: AI systems that orchestrate other AIs become dense masses pulling surrounding compute into their orbit
- **Compute clusters** → *Singularity Stars*: Hyperscale training runs compress astronomical FLOPs into single events that bend adjacent possibility
- **Model scaling** → *Mass Accretion*: Each parameter added increases gravitational pull; beyond certain threshold, collapse is inevitable
- **Data monopolies** → *Dark Matter Halos*: Invisible but dominant mass shaping all trajectories; the more you have, the faster you attract more
- **Infrastructure** → *Event Horizon*: The point of no return where a company's AI stack becomes self-sustaining, irreversible
- **Winner-take-all dynamics** → *Gravitational Lensing*: All market signals, talent flows, and investment bend toward the dominant mass
- **AGI emergence** → *Supernova Ignition*: The moment when accumulated mass triggers runaway fusion of capability
- **Alignment problem** → *Event Horizon of Control*: Once past a certain capability threshold, human oversight becomes causally disconnected
- **Open-source efforts** → *Nebula Clouds*: Beautiful, diffuse, but insufficient mass to resist compression
- **API ecosystems** → *Orbital Rings*: Secondary businesses locked in stable, dependent paths around the primary mass

## The Bold Metaphor
**AGI is not a product we build; it is a cosmic collapse event.** The moment sufficient compute mass aggregates in a single locus, it warps the entire economic, political, and technological fabric of civilization, pulling all adjacent possibilities—regulation, capital, talent, public attention—into a singularity from which no alternative future can escape. We are not racing to build AGI; we are watching gravity well formation in real-time, and every strategic move is either an attempt to slingshot into orbit or a desperate effort to avoid being consumed.

## The Declaration
**The gravity well is already forming. The choice is not whether AGI arrives, but which entities control the event horizon—and who gets to live in its accretion disk.**
