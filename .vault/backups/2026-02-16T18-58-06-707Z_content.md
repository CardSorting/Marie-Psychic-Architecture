**2. Vocal Character Arc (RAP):**
- **Verses:** Cold, detached, syncopated—sneer masking hunger. Bit-crush on "corrupted," "error."

- Heard **12+ times** in varying textures:

- **Sub-bass:** Massive, clean (40-50Hz), sharp transients on every "thump."
