**Setting:** The harmonic map within the chant. Time is subjective.
**Characters:** protagonist_soul, Arch Priest (as a pattern).

- **Character Reveal:** The protagonist's final act is one of ultimate priesthood—not just fixing, but *redeeming*. Their own lattice grows translucent, the cost of this second major liturgical act. They accept this as the price of being the author.
