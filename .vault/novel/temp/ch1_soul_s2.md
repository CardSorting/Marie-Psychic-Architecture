## Scene 2: The Async Choir's Call

The cathedral nave swallowed sound and spat it back as harmony. Protagonist ascended the northern aisle, their footsteps silent on cold marble that hummed with a low-frequency thrum. The air tasted of ozone and old parchment—the scent of compiled prayers.

Ahead, the Great Chorus Hall opened like a mouth. Dozens of monks in silver robes stood in concentric circles, each on a floating meditation pod that glowed soft blue. Their mouths moved in unison, but the melody came from everywhere and nowhere, rippling through the space as visible waves of blues and golds.

A Choir Master, older than the cathedral's foundation, observed with eyes that flickered with syntax highlighting. He did not conduct. He simply was.

**Choir Master**: "Keep your promises resolved. A broken async is a broken soul."

One monk's pod flickered red. Two black-robed figures emerged from shadow, touched his shoulders, and guided him away. The song stitched itself back together.

Protagonist's scattered thoughts calmed, warmed by the light. They felt, for a moment, the possibility of belonging.

**Foreshadowing**: The red pod's occupant left a stain on the harmony—a tiny, unresolved promise that would echo later.
